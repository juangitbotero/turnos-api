# ADR-003 — Revenue Model: Hybrid Subscription + Worker Fee

**Date:** May 2026
**Status:** ✅ Accepted

---

## Context

We evaluated three revenue models:
1. **Pure commission** — % on every transaction (Uber model)
2. **Pure subscription** — employers pay flat monthly fee
3. **Hybrid** — subscription + worker transaction fee

## Decision

**Hybrid model with two income streams:**

| Stream | Who Pays | Model | Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stream 1** | Employers | Monthly subscription | €99 / €199 / €299 /mo (3 tiers) |
| **Stream 2** | Workers | % per shift payout | **10%** of gross shift value |

The 10% worker fee:
- Is displayed clearly as *"Taxa de Serviço Turnos"* on every payslip
- Is lower than the EU platform norm of 10–15%, making it competitive
- At scale, even 8% is profitable when combined with subscription MRR

## Consequences

✅ Predictable MRR from subscriptions (easier to forecast)  
✅ Variable revenue scales with platform usage  
✅ Worker-friendly rate builds trust  
⚠️ Employers need to see value before committing to subscription  
⚠️ Worker fee must be clearly disclosed to avoid legal issues under Agenda do Trabalho Digno
