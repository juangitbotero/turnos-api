# ADR-002 — Launch City: Lisbon Beta Only

**Date:** May 2026
**Status:** ✅ Accepted

---

## Context

Turnos needs to validate its core loop (post shift → match worker → QR check-in → pay) before expanding geographically. Launching in multiple cities simultaneously would spread operations too thin and make it harder to identify what's working.

## Decision

**Closed beta in Lisbon only.**

Porto expansion only after beta metrics are validated:
- Fill rate > 70%
- NPS > 50

## Consequences

✅ Tighter operations control and faster feedback loops  
✅ Easier to build initial worker and employer supply in one city  
✅ Lower marketing spend in beta phase  
⚠️ Slower top-line growth in early months  
⚠️ Some employer prospects in Porto may need to wait
