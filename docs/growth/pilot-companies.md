# Pilot companies — who, how, list and email

Researched 2026-11-09. "Verified" = read on the business's own site that day.
"Directory" = from a tourism or listings site — confirm before sending.

## 1. Which businesses to pilot with

| Priority | Type | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Catering and event companies** | Staff needs change every week and every event; they already hire extras by phone and WhatsApp. Most shifts per company |
| 2 | **Restaurant, brunch and café groups with 3–10 venues** | Someone is always off sick or a venue is short on Saturday. One decision-maker covers several venues. Big enough to need it, small enough to decide in a week |
| 3 | **Boutique hotels and small hotel groups** | Housekeeping, breakfast service and events peak with occupancy |
| 4 | **Shops with a Christmas peak** | November–January: gift shops and markets need extra hands now |
| 5 | **Ericeira hotels and surf houses** | On your doorstep, so you can be there on the first shifts. Quiet now, strong from spring — good for relationships, weak for winter volume |

**Leave for later:** large chains (central purchasing, slow), single small
cafés (need extra staff too rarely to justify €45 a month), and anyone whose
need is permanent — the short contract Turnos is built on is for **peaks**, so
a company looking for a permanent part-timer is the wrong first customer.

## 2. The approach

1. **Workers first, in the same area and roles.** A company that posts a shift
   and gets no applicants will not post a second one. Before the first pilot
   shift, have 50–100 active profiles in Lisbon for waiter, bar, kitchen help,
   housekeeping. The training partners (see `partners-upskilling.md`) are the
   fastest source: their students want shifts.
2. **Five companies, not twenty.** Write to all 20, aim to start with 5. Two
   catering, two restaurant groups, one hotel.
3. **A founding-company offer.** Suggested: 3 months of Starter free and the
   €3 shift fee waived for the first 10 shifts, in exchange for feedback and a
   testimonial. Your call — it is a pricing decision.
4. **Be present.** Post the first shift with them on a call, go to the first
   check-in, phone the manager the next morning.
5. **Timing.** Company Christmas dinners, New Year's Eve and shop peaks start
   in three weeks. Send this week.
6. **What to measure:** shifts posted · share filled · hours from posting to
   a confirmed worker · no-shows · would they post again.

**Gates before a real pilot shift:** the public app (store approval), real SMS
sign-in (Twilio), and Stripe live before any company pays.

## 4. Email to companies

**Subject:** Reforços para os picos de dezembro — piloto Turnos

> Bom dia [Nome],
>
> Sou o Juan Botero, fundador da **Turnos** (turnos.cc), uma aplicação
> portuguesa para preencher turnos curtos em [catering / restauração /
> hotelaria].
>
> Funciona assim: publica o turno (função, horário, valor por hora), recebe
> candidaturas de trabalhadores com perfil, foto e avaliações, e escolhe quem
> quer. No dia, a pessoa faz o check-in com um código QR à entrada. A
> contratação e o pagamento são feitos diretamente por si; a Turnos trata da
> procura e deixa-lhe prontos os dados de cada contratação.
>
> Estamos a escolher **5 empresas fundadoras** em Lisboa para um piloto. Para
> essas empresas: [3 meses sem mensalidade e os primeiros 10 turnos sem taxa],
> acompanhamento direto meu, e a possibilidade de moldar o produto.
>
> Com os jantares de Natal e a passagem de ano a chegar, faz sentido falarmos?
> Bastam 15 minutos, por telefone ou aí no espaço.
>
> Obrigado,
> Juan Botero
> Turnos · NESTS ALLEY, LDA
> contact@turnos.cc · turnos.cc

**Short version for Instagram or WhatsApp:**

> Olá! Sou o Juan, fundador da Turnos (turnos.cc), uma app portuguesa para
> encontrar reforços para turnos curtos em restauração e hotelaria. Estamos a
> escolher 5 espaços em Lisboa para um piloto gratuito antes do Natal. Com quem
> posso falar 10 minutos sobre isto?

Wording to keep: the company hires and pays; Turnos never says it guarantees
or processes the payment, and never calls workers freelancers.
