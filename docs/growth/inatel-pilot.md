# INATEL — pilot proposal

Warm lead: Juanes met Mr. Serrano and Ms. Sachim before launch; they were
waiting for the apps to be public. INATEL runs 15 hotels. Proposed pilot:
**INATEL Oeiras and/or INATEL Caparica** (closest to Lisbon, where the worker
pool is). One-pager: `turnos-inatel-pilot.pdf` (source
`one-pager/inatel-pilot.html`). Written 2026-11-11.

## Email

**Subject:** Turnos × INATEL — pilot proposal

> Dear Mr. Serrano, dear Ms. Sachim,
>
> Thank you for your patience. Apple and Google have now approved Turnos, and
> the app is live on both stores — so we are ready to start the pilot we
> discussed.
>
> **Our proposal**
> - **Where:** INATEL Oeiras and/or INATEL Caparica. Being close to Lisbon,

> - **How:** your team posts the shifts it needs (housekeeping, restaurant and
>   bar, kitchen, reception, events); workers nearby apply; your team chooses
>   who to hire. We will be with you from the first shift.
>
> **What we need from you**
> 1. The contact at each hotel (ideally the manager or whoever organises the
>    staff), so we can show them how to post a shift and use the dashboard —
>    it takes about 30 minutes.
> 2. One person we can go to for any issue or feedback during the pilot.
>
> You will find the details in the attached one-page summary. You can also
> see the platform at turnos.cc, and the worker app at turnos.cc/app.

**What changed from the draft, and why:**
- The apology is one line and turns positive at once ("ready to start").
- Location, cost and how it works are three short bullets — easy to forward
  internally.
- The two asks are numbered, so the reply can be "1. X, 2. Y".
- "Completely free" is stated without the worry that it sounds too good:
  "your time and feedback in return" makes it a fair deal.
