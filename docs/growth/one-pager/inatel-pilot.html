What Turnos is

A Portuguese app for short shifts. The hotel posts the shift it needs; workers nearby who do that job get a notification and apply. Your team chooses who to hire. Live on the App Store and Google Play since November 2026.

Roles you can fill

Housekeeping · restaurant and bar · kitchen and dishwashing · reception · events and banqueting — for a single shift or several days in a row.

How a shift works

1

Your team posts the shift on turnos.cc: role, date, hours and hourly pay.

2

Nearby workers with that role are notified and apply from the app.

3

You see each candidate's photo, experience and reviews, and pick one.

4

On the day, the worker checks in with the hotel's QR code at the door.

Built for Portuguese rules

  • Workers are 18+, with a profile photo checked at the door
  • Short-term contract limits and 11h rest are checked automatically
  • Hire data ready to export for your Social Security filing
  • The hotel is the employer and pays the worker directly

What INATEL gets

  • Faster cover for peaks, events and last-minute absences
  • A pool of reviewed workers you can call on again
  • A say in how the product develops
  • No cost and no commitment after the pilot

What we ask

  • A contact at each hotel (the manager or whoever organises staff) — a 30-minute setup together
  • One person for feedback and any issue during the pilot
  • Your honest feedback after the first shifts
